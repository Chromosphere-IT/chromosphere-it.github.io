Support (English)

BinaryClockWatch shows the time as a watch face complication in 13 different number systems, as a standalone Apple Watch app.

Questions, problems or feedback? Write to ckdevel@me.com. For bugs, please include your watch model, watchOS version and the affected complication.

BinaryClockWatch collects no data. See the privacy policy.
